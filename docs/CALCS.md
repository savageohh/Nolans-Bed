## Assumptions worth knowing

- Vertical loads go into the posts through **wood bearing** on the routed housing shoulders. The bolts and
  inserts only hold the joint closed and resist racking; they are not counted for vertical capacity.
- A point load on the deck goes mostly to the nearest rib (60% at 12in spacing); the mattress and plywood
  spread the rest. Plywood-to-rib composite action is ignored, so the ribs are conservative.
- Sway is calculated with the end-frame mortise-and-tenon joints at 50% of fully rigid. Glued M&T joints
  usually do better than that.
- Insert withdrawal allowable (300 lb, 3/8-16 in softwood face grain) is a conservative assumption. Do the
  pull test in DESIGN.md on a scrap of *your* lumber with *your* inserts before trusting it.
- This is a careful hobbyist analysis, not a stamped engineering design or a certification to ASTM F1427.
