## Ways to spend less

- Buy lumber during a Menards 11% rebate week, or in the lumber sales flyer.
- Use a zinc 50-pack of 3/8-16 inserts instead of E-Z Hex 25-packs. Do the pull test from DESIGN.md first.
- Buy the spare boards, then return the ones you don't use (keep the receipt). That's about $50–80.
- Already applied: OSB deck, 1x8 pine end panels, a diagonal brace instead of a back panel,
  one insert size, felt pads instead of glides, and finish only on visible parts. Glue and
  sandpaper are assumed on hand.
- Buy bolts by the box instead of from the bin. Box pricing often runs half the per-piece bin price.
